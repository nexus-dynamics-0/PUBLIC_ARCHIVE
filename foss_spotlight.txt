             🐧 FOSS CELL GECT : THE HEARTBEAT OF OPEN SOURCE 🐧

Welcome to the open world!

Here at Government Engineering College Thrissur (GECT), code isn't just about
passing unit tests or finishing assignments—it's about freedom, community,
and the unstoppable power of collaborative creation. That is the soul of
FOSS Cell GECT (Free and Open Source Software Cell).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE STAND FOR:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 🔓 Freedom to Inspect: No walled gardens, no black-box secrets. Open code
  means everyone has the right to peek under the hood and learn how things work.
* 🛠️ Freedom to Build: From scripting automation tools to contributing to the
  Linux kernel, we believe in learning by breaking, fixing, and building.
* 🤝 Community First: Knowledge multiplies when shared. Senior hackers guide
  first-year coders, pull requests turn into mentorships, and weekend hackathons
  turn into lifelong friendships.
* 🌐 Global Impact: When you push code to an open-source project, you're not
  just coding for yourself—you're contributing to a worldwide digital commons
  used by millions of developers and humans around the globe.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FOSS CULTURE AT GECT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Whether it's organizing Linux Install Fests, diving deep into Git workflows,
hosting hands-on FOSS workshops, or rallying late-night hack squads, FOSS Cell
GECT is the proving ground where curiosity meets real-world software engineering.

We believe that the best engineers aren't created by memorizing syntax in a vacuum;
they're forged in the heat of open-source repositories, collaborating across
timezones, solving tricky merge conflicts, and celebrating every accepted PR!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A QUICK CALL TO ACTION FOR EVERY TREASURE HUNTER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Don't just be a consumer of technology—be a creator.
2. Star an open-source repository that helped you learn.
3. Open an issue, fix a typo in the documentation, or submit your first PR!
4. Join the FOSS Cell GECT circle, share what you learn, and keep the open-source
   spirit thriving.

Remember: Proprietary code hides the gears. Open Source hands you the wrench.

Long live FOSS, long live Linux, and keep on hacking! 🚀
